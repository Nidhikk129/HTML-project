The Beginning

X's & O's started in 2019 as a small coffee cart parked outside the farmers market. Our regulars kept asking where they could find us after dark, so in 2023 we opened our first real home on Sunset Lane.

We wanted a place that felt as comfortable at 8 AM with a latte as it does at 9 PM with a cocktail.

Day & Night

The X's (Day)

Bright, sunny, and full of plants. We serve specialty coffee, tea, fresh pastries, and all-day brunch.

The O's (Night)

Low lights, velvet booths, and a purple glow. We serve craft cocktails, local beer and wine, and small plates.
